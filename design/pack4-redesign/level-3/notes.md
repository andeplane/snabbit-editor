# Breathing Cove — Pack 4 Level 3 / ID 4004

The built-in image generation tool produced the concept before any map was authored. `concept.png` is its unchanged output and `prompt.json` contains the exact submitted prompt and both reference paths. Studied actual first three authored levels of packs 10, 20, 30 (IDs 168, 205, 138; 214, 141, 163; 236, 238, 240), then inspected all three opening contact sheets plus the actual sprite/lever reference.

The map translates the concept's lower-left crescent, lower-right and middle-left star coves, loose fragments, left-side blinking petal and final upper island. It uses a 16-column offset grid and 74 rows. `native-map.png` is a deterministic original-sprite rendering of the JSON; `concept-vs-json.png` places it beside the unchanged concept. The native sprites are smaller than the imagegen illustration, but the landmarks and negative space match. There are 149 obstacle cells in 1184 cells (12.6%); no full-width wall row, continuous side rail, repeated zigzag or long narrow corridor. Seven gems are occasional glints, not a rail. Three finish cells occupy the small final opening; the renderer shows individual cyan points, not a full-width bar.

The intended discovery route starts at native (row 0, col 9), drifting into lever A at (10,10). It opens two timed stones at (12,10) and (13,10). Star 1 lies in the right-hand cove at (17,12). Drift left through the loose fragments to lever B at (33,5), cross its two diagonal gate stones at (34,5) and (35,4), collect star 2 at (38,5), then use the large open gap to drift back right through the upper island and collect star 3 at (61,9). Finish at row 67. The five-cell pulse petal at rows 47–51 is an optional visual surprise off to the left, not a mandatory timed choke.

The four linked stones use native timer `[1,40,40,8,0,0]` (row delay is index 3), so their timer state is ON at native `8*row` arrival. Flipping the correct lever XORs that state to OFF. Their visible state still pulses before/after arrival. The separate petal uses `[0,32,40,0,4,8]` and is not linked to either switch. The supplied proof checks effective collision HP at every gate arrival, with and without the corresponding bit.

Validation ran `importLevel`, `solve` and `advanceRun` from the actual shared editor core. `validation-report.json` contains the full replay path, source hashes, events and gate crossings. The optimal zero-damage route collects all three stars, both levers and all four linked gate cells: 1490 score, 15 turns, 3 lives retained, 0 crashes, 544 ticks, 2024 expanded states. The route was replayed with each row, column and time checked. This is shared-core simulation evidence; physical-device play remains for the parent/native validation pass.

No commit, push, installation or shared-file edit was performed. All artifacts are inside this level-3 directory.
